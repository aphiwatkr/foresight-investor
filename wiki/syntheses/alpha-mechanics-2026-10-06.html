2. The mechanic stack (what to actually run)

Layer 1 — Alpha core (LONG only): QAE book (monthly, invvol-weighted) or the simpler 12-1
momentum top decile. Both live-validated in-repo; run monthly, cost ~10bps × 0.33 turnover.

Layer 2 — Leading-indicator gate: composite ≥2 → full risk-on; ≤1 → cut book to 50% and raise
cash (T-bills 4.3% pays you to wait). This converts −35% tails into −14% tails at zero CAGR cost —
it is what makes modest leverage survivable.

Layer 3 — Leverage ≤1.5× (futures/margin, financing 5.3%): realistic outcome band 55-75% CAGR,
maxDD ~40-50% — the highest survivable gear this data supports. 2×+ = ruin math.

Layer 4 — Right tail the leverage can't buy: the ic-book-2026-10-05 100x sleeve (14 names,
≤0.5% each) — convexity from 9/9-scored microcaps (FENC/ASIC tier), not from leverage.

Layer 5 — Shorts, correctly used: never naked momentum-losers (tested: they drift up). Only
(a) index/beta shorts when composite ≤1 (hedging Layer 1), (b) event-driven single-name shorts on
Δ-layer invalidations (e.g., GRAL denial-type binaries), sized ≤1% each with borrow 5.3% priced in.

The honesty ledger (read before believing any number above)

  1. Survivorship bias, quantified: the 332-name universe was selected in 2026 from themes that
    already won — EW universe beats SPY by +7.6%/yr for free. That artifact flows into every
    variant (momentum concentrates the fastest winners inside a winner-picked pool → 2020/2024's
    +118-130% years are fantasy-adjacent). Deflated expectation: subtract ≥8-15pts from every CAGR
    → realistic book+gate ≈ 35-50%, V3 ≈ 50-60%, V5 ≈ 65-75% — IF the factor edge itself
    survives outside the biased pool.
  2. What IS bias-robust (level-independent): the DELTAS. Gate vs no-gate: +8.5pts CAGR and maxDD
    −27.8→−19.5 at lower vol. Hedge-short vs no hedge in bear years: 2018 +36.7 vs V2's +13ish, 2022
    +62.8 — defensive structure works regardless of universe. The ordering V3>V2>V1 and the gate's
    tail-cutting are the transferable findings.
  3. Look-ahead audit: 12m→12-1 fix lowered V1 by 2.6pts only (the study's other numbers unchanged);
    monthly signals act on next bar; no same-bar fills.
  4. Regime note: even unbiased, the window contains the 2025-26 supercycle.
