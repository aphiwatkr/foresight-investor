Dual-model shortlist scorecard — 2026-06-03

Bottom line: Eight representative securities from cross-theme-idea-ranking run through both engines on 2026-06-03, as a dated, falsifiable scorecard to validate later (review 2026-09-03). The Futurist model = thematic conviction (1–5, risk-adjusted, insight only). The MDAEM model = Aphiwat's framework — 10 hard gates → 10 weighted dims → tier → SA-Quant overlay → scenario math → verdict + indicative sizing (the operator's tool; decisions remain the user's responsibility).

Data note: Figures pulled live 2026-06-03 from stockanalysis.com / Seeking Alpha / company filings (per-name sources in the ingest log). Several SA Quant 5-factor letter grades were paywalled → marked n/f; no figures fabricated. Prices are intraday 2026-06-03.

The key finding (why run two models)

The Futurist model rates every theme here 3.5–4 (all attractive megatrends). MDAEM rejects 3 of the 8 — CRWD, GEV, IBM — not on the business, but on entry: each fails the asymmetry or revisions gate (consensus targets at/below price, or negative EPS-revision skew). This is the radar's own warning — high trend conviction ≠ attractive entry today — turned into hard verdicts. The two models agree only where a great theme also clears the discipline: TSM and LLY.
