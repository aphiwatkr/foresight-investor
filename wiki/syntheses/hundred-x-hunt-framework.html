The 100x Hunt — A Framework for Finding the Next Decade's 100-Baggers

Bottom line: 100x in 10 years = 58.5% CAGR — it is a power-law lottery ticket that must be
bought structurally, not found by luck: tiny base + category-error mislabeling + proven unit
economics + monopoly-in-disguise + owner-operator + funded runway, held coffee-can style in a
20-30 name basket where 1-2 survivors carry everything. Below: the evidence from our own 10-year
price data, the pre-rise signature (ช่วงที่หุ้นยังไม่ขึ้น), the masters' lenses, the 5-pillar
scoring rubric, and today's (2026-10-03) scored candidate basket. Own-book discipline: this is
a research framework, never advice; the sleeve it justifies is small and loss-tolerant.

1. The math nobody escapes

Horizon 100x requires Real-world shape
10y 58.5%/yr almost always starts < $3B cap
12y 47%/yr start < $5B typical
15y 35%/yr quality compounder can do it from $10-20B (rare)
20y 26%/yr the Buffett/Mayer path — the easy 100x is just TIME × ROIC

Decomposition — a 100x needs all three factors visible ex-ante:
100x ≈ Revenue 25–50× (TAM capture) × Margin 3–6× (operating leverage: 5%→30%+ op margin) × Multiple 1.5–2× (at most)
(inference — arithmetic identity applied to historical cases). If your candidate needs the
multiple to do 10x of the work, it is a bubble bet, not a 100x bet.

3. The pre-rise signature (ลายเซ็น "ช่วงหุ้นยังไม่ขึ้น") — 7 marks

⚠ Empirically re-graded 2026-10-29 by hundredx-anatomy-2026-10-29 (22 winners ≥20x mined
from our own cache + SEC XBRL, vs controls): marks 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 carry corrections — at the base
you cannot see growth or profitability (F2); the inflection appears in the launch year (F3);
"GM ≥40%" applies to the software path only — the hardware path rides 15–25% GM (F5); "funded"
includes dilution-as-fuel and TSLA-style leverage (F4); and the $25M ADV line moved out of Gate 0
into sizing, because it would have excluded half the cohort at their troughs (F7). The marks below
are the original 2026-10-03 text, kept for the record; the anatomy's table in §6 of that page is
the operative version.

Every case above shows most of these before the liftoff:

  1. Category error (ถูกจัดหมวกผิด). The market prices the OLD business while the new engine is
    quietly becoming the majority: NVDA "gaming" (datacenter % climbing), CLS "contract mfg" (AI
    servers), Axon "taser" (evidence.com SaaS), DELTA.BK "Thai electronics" (AI power/cooling).
    This is the single richest tell — the 100x is priced as a 2x.
  2. Small & unwatched. Start <$3B (10y) or <$10B (15y); <10 analysts; institutional ownership
    low; no index forced-buying. QUBT/SEZL/LEU all fit. (Bessembinder (b): ~4% of listed stocks
    create ALL net equity wealth — the tail IS the market.)
  3. Business inflects before the price. Revenue accelerating 30%+ at small base, or margin
    inflecting (AMD Zen, ENPH IQ7), gross margin ≥40% and stable-to-rising, R&D ≥15% of sales —
    the operating leverage is loaded but unreleased (opex growth << revenue growth).
  4. Monopoly in disguise. ≥40% niche share; network/data/switching-cost/regulatory moat the
    market models as commodity; today's TAM looks small but is on a 10-100× S-curve <10%
    penetrated (Thiel's "secret" — true but not consensus).
  5. Owner-operator + reflexive fuel. Founder-led with ≥10% skin (Jensen, Musk, Su, Mark
    Leonard), proven capital allocation, and the Soros loop available: rising price → cheap
    equity currency → faster compounding. The pre-rise window usually contains one near-death
    scare     that washed out weak hands (Tesla '08/'18, NFLX '11, AMD '15, LEU '18, Priceline '01).
  6. Funded to the inflection. Net cash or ≥3y runway; burn buys measurable share/S-curve
    progress. (Not pre-revenue story paper — that's a lottery option, sized differently.)
  7. Price hasn't told the story. A 12-36 month flat/down base — often −50-75% drawdown — while
    fundamentals improve underneath (the divergence); EV/S materially below growth-matched peers.

What a 100x is NOT: cyclical peak margins (2021 shipping = 10x round-trip), levered balance
sheets, story SPACs without unit economics, and — hardest — the already-discovered theme
darling up 30x (the second 10x is easy; the second 100x isn't).

4. The masters, one line each (what each lens contributes)

  • Buffett/Munger: quality × runway × time. You cannot screen for 100x; you screen for
    durable compounding and let it become 100x. ("Our holding period is forever" = the math above.)
  • Dalio: the paradigm machine — 100x ride the NEXT paradigm while consensus extrapolates the
    current one. Candidates for 2026-2036: AI physicalization (robotics/autonomy), energy abundance
    (nuclear/grid), longevity bio, sovereign digital rails, space economy. (macro-regime overlay.)
  • Soros: reflexivity + "first survive." Prefer companies that can USE a rising price as fuel
    (equity currency, talent magnetism) — and size so no single zero kills you.
  • Lynch: the earnings line — a 10y 100x needs a credible EPS path of 35%+/yr; go see the unit
    economics (scuttlebutt), not the slide deck.
  • Thiel: monopoly + secret. Contrarian AND right — one without the other is a value trap or a bubble.
  • Marks: cycle position. The best entries feel awful (base/drawdown, "it will never work");
    if it feels comfortable, you're late.
  • Bessembinder (the academic): power-law outcomes → basket construction, not conviction
    concentration, at ENTRY.

5. THE FRAMEWORK — Gate 0 + five pillars (score each 0/1/2; pass ≥7/10)

Gate 0 (structural, all must pass): market cap <$10B (hard cap $20B for 15y horizon) ·
non-biotech OR explicitly sized as a binary option · net cash or ≥3y funded runway · liquid
enough for the sleeve (>$25M daily $).

Pillar 0 pts 2 pts
P1 S-curve regime (Dalio) end-of-cycle demand sits on a <10%-penetrated $100B+ forced-spend wave (AI-physical, nuclear, longevity, space, digital rails); wave timing NOW (capex confirmed, revenue not)
P2 Monopoly in disguise (Thiel/Buffett) commodity share ≥40% niche share or credible path; moat (data/network/switching/regulatory) still priced as commodity; GM ≥50% or +500bps/yr
P3 Operator + reflexive fuel (Soros) hired gun, <2% ownership founder ≥10% + proven allocator + equity-currency loop available; survived a near-death event
P4 Pre-inflection financials (Lynch) flat revenue, no margin path revenue ≥30%/yr at small base (or margin inflection visible); GM ≥40%; R&D ≥15% sales; opex growth << revenue growth; unit economics proven at small scale
P5 Obscurity + category error (Marks) crowded consensus name <10 analysts; the market files it in the OLD bucket while new engine grows; 12-36m base or −50%+ drawdown with intact fundamentals

Portfolio rules (the part that actually generates the 100x):
20-30 names · 0.25-0.5% each at entry (sleeve 5-8% of book max, inside portfolio-blueprint
caps) · expect 60-80% of names to round-trip to ~0 · the math only needs 1×100x + a few 3-10x ·
pre-commit: never trim the winner before year 8 unless thesis breaks or >70% of addressable
TAM captured · quarterly re-score; add only on P-score improvement, not price · every name carries
a written invalidation.

7. Behavior — the framework's real edge

Mayer's data (b): the median 100x took 26 years and >50% of holders earned <10x on the same
stock by trading it. The rubric finds candidates; the coffee-can creates the 100x. Rules:
entries are small and boring; adds only on score improvement; no price-based trimming of winners;
annual re-read of the thesis, not the chart.
