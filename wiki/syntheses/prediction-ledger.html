Prediction Ledger

Bottom line: A dated, falsifiable record of the engine's calls — and how they turn out.
This is the accountability layer: it lets the "Futurist Investor" engine measure its own
hit-rate and calibration, instead of quietly forgetting wrong calls. Insight only — not advice.

Rules. Every prediction is (1) dated, (2) falsifiable with a concrete condition +
resolve-by date, (3) tagged with conviction (1–5) at the time, (4) later scored:
✅ correct · ❌ wrong · ◐ partial · ⏳ open. Never edit the original call — add the verdict in
its own column. Score matured calls during each scan.
