4. The Q4 operating rules (what changed and why)

Factor view (trades) vs macro view (context only) — per post-mortem-2026-10-03, discretionary macro does
not trade in a momentum-confirmed tape. Only the measured gate below scales exposure.

  1. Systematic core = Engine A (xs_mom_top10_iv_vt15, WINNER in both the 09-06 and 10-11 tournaments, Sharpe
    1.12, maxDD −20%). Ten names at 3.5% of the engine each, 15% vol target, rebalanced 11-04, 12-04, 2027-01-06
    (the 10-06 rebalance was not filed — execute the current ten at the next session).
  2. Weekly gate (measured, lag-correct): at each Friday close, score ≤1 → hold the momentum book at half
    weight     the next week; ≥2 → full. Backtest (2017-12 → 2026-10): Sharpe 1.45 → 1.47, worst year −14.0% → +0.3%,
    ≈ −3 pts CAGR — insurance, not alpha. Currently ≤1 → half weight.
  3. No hedge-short, no leverage. Corrected, the hedge lowers Sharpe (1.33) and 1.25–1.5× gives maxDD −33 to −39%
    on a survivorship-flattered universe.
  4. Engine B (DBC) demoted. All dual-momentum ensembles lost winner status. Step DBC 50% → 25% at 11-04 → 0% at
    12-04     unless the December tournament restores it; freed capital to T-bills (BIL ≈ 4.3%). P27/P28 keep testing
    the v2 system as filed.
  5. Confirmation rule: full weight only when momentum (QAE top-15 / Engine A) and FFM ≥60 ungated agree;
    momentum-only names run at half weight with the 2.5×ATR stop; FFM ≥73 ungated outside the momentum book =
    accumulate on pullback.
  6. Earnings rule: no new full-size entry inside 10 sessions of a print; stage ⅓ before, ⅔ after a confirming print.
  7. 100x sleeve rules: ≤0.5% per name, staged thirds; no initiation above the Street mean target or >100% above
    the 200-dma    ; add only after the next print confirms the memo's key metric.
  8. Caps (portfolio-blueprint): name ≤8%, theme ≤12%, AI-capex cluster ≤25%, cash ≥3%.
