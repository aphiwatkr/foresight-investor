Why this cluster matters now (the diversification read)

The theme-linkage-map and bubble-implications-by-theme both flag defense as the intentional
diversifier — it has the lowest correlation to the AI-capex engine that dominates the rest of
the radar. In a reflexive-break scenario (regime-monitor Act 2), defense is the cluster that
holds up. This is why portfolio-blueprint weights it 8–10% regardless of the AI stance.

What would break the cluster

  • A sustained peace dividend (e.g., a durable Russia-Ukraine settlement plus a Taiwan detente) —
    would compress defense multiples. Low probability; not the base case.
  • US fiscal austerity hitting the Pentagon budget — politically fraught but a tail risk.
  • For critical-minerals: a demand-side miss (EV transition slows) or China dumping the market.

Confidence & gaps

    confirmed via SIPRI primary source.
  • 5%-GDP target: a proposal (Hague 2025), not yet ratified budgets — treat as upside optionality,
    not in the base case.
