Strategy tournament — 39 systematic strategies, 10 years, split-sample selection

Update 2026-10-11 (quarterly re-run, window 2017-10-20 → 2026-10-09): the proposed v2 system
ens_R2_top10vt15+dualdef is no longer a WINNER — it still beat SPY in half 2 but fell out of
half 1's top quartile (full Sharpe 0.985 → 0.877). All three dual-momentum ensembles lost winner
status; all six winners are now pure cross-sectional momentum (Engine A xs_mom_top10_iv_vt15:
Sharpe 1.118, winner both runs). A one-month window shift flipping the verdict is itself a fragility
finding. P27/P28 stay tracked as filed. Q4 consequence: q4-action-plan-2026-10-11 §4.

Bottom line: Of 39 pre-registered systematic strategies run over 2017-09 → 2026-09 with
costs, six passed the two-gate protocol (top-quartile Sharpe on the first half and beat
SPY on Sharpe on the second half they were not selected on). All six are variants of one
idea: concentrated cross-sectional momentum with the position size set by volatility,
diversified with an asset-class dual-momentum leg. The proposed system —
ens_R2_top10vt15+dualdef — delivered +18.2% CAGR at 15.7% vol, Sharpe 0.98, max drawdown
−23% against SPY's +15.1% / 18.8% / 0.71 / −33.7%, was up +9.6% in the 2022 bear when
SPY fell 18%, and held a Sharpe of 0.99 → 0.98 across the two halves. It survives a 25 bps
cost assumption. It does not clear the multiple-testing luck band — nothing does — and the
stock leg sits on a survivor-biased universe. Both are quantified below.

Scope. Research on the operator's own methodology, never advice to others. Every figure
is produced by scripts/tournament.py from scripts/_hist/prices_10y.parquet

1. Why a tournament, and why this protocol

quant-alpha-methodology v1 measured factors one at a time and found one honest signal
(momentum). The next question a desk asks is not "which factor" but "which system" —
signal + construction + risk overlay + diversifier — and that can only be answered by
running many complete systems on the same days with the same costs and then applying a
selection rule that was written before looking at the results.

The rule: a strategy is a winner only if it is (a) top-quartile by Sharpe in
2017-09-18 → 2022-03-10 and (b) beats SPY's Sharpe in 2022-03-10 → 2026-09-04.
Stock-picking strategies must also (c) beat the equal-weight of their own universe in the
confirmation half, because that universe is survivor-biased (§5). Everything else is a
candidate, not a result.

Simulator honesty: daily P&L with weight drift; signals use data through close t, trades
fill at t+1; 10 bps per side on every unit of turnover; cash earns the T-bill ETF (BIL);
gross never exceeds 100%. One look-ahead bug was caught and fixed during the build (the
realized-vol scaler was reading its own future curve); the bugged version had produced a
"winner" that vanished once fixed. That is the whole reason to run the thing twice.

4. What the exploration taught (in order of how much it changes behaviour)

  1. Volatility targeting is the largest single improvement, and it is free. The same
    ten momentum names run unmanaged: Sharpe 0.99, drawdown −36%. With gross exposure scaled
    to a 15% ex-ante vol: Sharpe 1.03, drawdown −18.6%, and the 2020 crash becomes −5.5%
    instead of −19.5% for SPY. Nothing about the selection changed.
  2. Concentration paid. Top-10 beat top-20 beat top-30 on every metric (Sharpe 0.99 →
    0.87 → 0.74). The signal is strongest in the tail of the ranking; diluting it with names 11–30
    adds beta, not alpha. (Note the 8% single-name cap binds on all ten names, so "top-10
    inverse-vol" is in practice ten equal 8% weights at 80% gross — the vol-target version then
    scales that.)
  3. Diversify across engines, not across more names. The blend with dual momentum has a
    lower Sharpe than the stock engine alone (0.98 vs 1.03) but a far more stable one across
    halves (0.99/0.98 vs 0.87/1.20), a lower beta (0.51), and it was positive in 2022. Stability
    across regimes is what "winning again" actually requires.
  4. Theme-ETF rotation failed badly. Every variant had a negative alpha vs SPY and
    −8% to −16% vs the equal-weight universe. Buying last season's hot theme at 9–13× annual
    turnover is the opposite of the vault's multi-year thematic approach — and the tournament
    says the tactical version does not work. This is the most useful negative result here.
  5. Long-only time-series momentum and risk parity were weak in this decade (Sharpe
    0.3–0.44). Bonds spent 2022 falling with equities, which removes the diversifier these
    systems rely on. TSMOM earns its place only as the third leg of an ensemble.
  6. The ridge model did not beat the hand-weighted composite (0.93 vs 0.99) and failed the
    equal-weight test in half 2. Fourteen noisy factors and two years of training rows is not
    where machine learning wins.
  7. The 200-dma regime gate hurt (0.63 vs 0.87). It exits after the fall and re-enters after
    the rebound. Scaling by volatility beat switching on direction.

5. What is still wrong with these numbers (read before believing them)

Survivorship bias is large and quantified. The equal-weight of the tracked universe
returned +25.2%/yr; the equal-weight S&P 500 ETF (RSP), which has no hindsight in it,
returned +11.8%/yr. That ~13-point gap is the cost of building a universe from names that
are interesting today. Consequences:
- Absolute CAGRs of every stock-selection strategy are inflated. Do not quote them.
- The α vs EW-universe column is the honest one for stock engines: the proposed system's
+6.7%/yr and the stock engine's +5.5%/yr survive that benchmark. That is the momentum
effect within the universe, net of the universe's own bias.
- The dual-momentum and TSMOM legs use broad ETFs (SPY, QQQ, INDA, GLD, DBC, VNQ, bonds) that
exist regardless of hindsight — they are essentially free of this bias.

Multiple testing. With 39 trials over 8.9 years, the best of 39 zero-skill strategies
would show a Sharpe about +1.01 above SPY by luck (SE of a Sharpe estimate ≈ 0.375). The
proposed system's edge is +0.27. No result here is statistically distinguishable from luck
at the family-max level. The split-sample gate is the mitigation, not a cure; the real
defence is that the winning components are the two most-replicated effects in the literature
(cross-sectional momentum; absolute/relative momentum across asset classes) and the
construction rules (vol targeting, concentration) have theory behind them.

Momentum's known tail. The 2009-style momentum crash — a violent bear-market rebound in
which the losers you are not holding double — did not occur in this window. The vol target
and the 8% cap reduce it; nothing removes it.

One regime for the diversifier. Dual momentum's half-2 Sharpe fell to 0.63; its 2026 YTD
is −8.2% (it rotated into commodities as they peaked). This is the leg most likely to
disappoint next.

6. The proposed methodology (what "win again" looks like in rules)

Two engines, each 50% of capital, rebalanced on the same day every 21 trading days.

Engine A — Momentum-10, vol-targeted (xs_mom_top10_iv_vt15)
1. Universe: quant-alpha-methodology Stage-1 filters (liquidity, history, structural-decay
and vol-collapse guards). Today: 270 names.
2. Score = 0.5 × z(12-1 momentum) + 0.5 × z(126d trend quality), z-scored within stocks/ETFs.
3. Take the top 10 with at most 3 per theme; weight ∝ 1/σ₆₃ (floored at 60% of 1y vol), cap 8%.
4. Scale the whole leg so ex-ante vol = 15% (ρ = 0.5); remainder in T-bills. Never levered.
5. Per-name stop 2.5 × ATR(20). Book-level ladder −5% / −8% / −12% as in v1.

Engine B — Dual momentum with a defensive-momentum leg (dual_mom_broad_def)
1. Candidates: SPY, QQQ, INDA, GLD, DBC, VNQ.
2. Hold the single candidate with the highest trailing-12-month return if it beats
T-bills' 12-month return.
3. Otherwise hold the best 12-month performer among IEF, TLT, GLD, BIL.

Risk-averse variant: add a third engine (multi-asset 1/3/6/12-month trend, 10% vol target)
in equal thirds — Sharpe 0.92, max DD −21.4%, 2022 drawdown −7.6%, at the cost of ~4 points of
CAGR.

Invalidation of the system (written now, before it runs live):
- Engine A: the 12-1 momentum factor's rolling 12-month rank IC turns negative → Engine A to
T-bills until it recovers.
- Engine B: two consecutive years underperforming a 60/40 by >5 points → replace with the
3-engine variant.
- System: a −15% peak-to-trough drawdown, which the backtest never produced, is treated as
evidence the regime has changed, not as a dip to buy.
